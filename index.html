The Sport

The world's only
3D team sport

Underwater Rugby is a fast-paced, full-contact sport played in three dimensions — under the surface, around the goal, and through your limits. Two teams of six battle it out at the bottom of a deep pool, using breath control, strategy, and trust.

It's intense, it's global, and it's unlike anything you've ever played.

Life at AUWR

Our Story

About AUWR

01
Origins

How It Started

A small group of passionate players wanting to bring something different to the water. Over the years, a diverse international community of athletes who meet weekly to train, compete, and have fun.

"Breath In, Breath Out. Let your body do what your body is capable of doing"

— Wim Hof (Indirect Team Breathing Coach)
02
Training

What We're About

We train twice a week, compete nationally and internationally, and welcome all levels. Beginner or elite — you'll find support, laughs, and serious gameplay.

"You know, Concussions"

— Terry
03
Culture

The AUWR Spirit

We take our game seriously — but never ourselves. Late-night memes, sideline banter, shared rides to comps. Here to win, but more importantly, to grow as a team and as people.

"It is normal to feel pain with the fins, what is not normal is to complain that much, Ricardo"

— Callum
